# Account Review

1. Get the account record and relevant support tickets.
2. List observable risk factors, including severity of open tickets and renewal proximity.
3. Do not infer customer sentiment without evidence.
4. Suggest a prioritized, non-destructive action plan.
5. If an account-status change is requested, use the approval-protected tool.
