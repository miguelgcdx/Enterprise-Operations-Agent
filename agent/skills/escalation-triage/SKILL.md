# Escalation Triage

1. Verify the account and ticket IDs.
2. State severity, current status, and reported customer impact.
3. Identify missing facts and who should investigate them.
4. Create a short internal action plan with owner, priority, and next check.
5. Do not take an external action without the appropriate approval.
